\noindent\textbf{Interpretation:} The autonomy frame increases willingness under low burden (+0.74) and decreases willingness under high burden ($-$4.22), resulting in a net positive delta (+4.96) that indicates stronger burden effects when autonomy framing is present. The collective frame shows the opposite pattern: it slightly increases low-burden willingness (+0.41) but substantially increases high-burden willingness (+11.29), producing a large negative delta ($-$10.88) consistent with reduced burden effects under collective obligation framing.
